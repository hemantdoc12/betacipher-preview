Key features of BetaCipher

1

Start from Home

Statements grouped by account holder, with each account and its bank under the holder. Start a review from there.

2

One account, in depth

Channel, entity, party ledger, period and amount views for a single account. The period chart opens by month, quarter or year to suit the span. Click a figure to see the statement rows behind it.

3

Entities you can correct

Similar names can be grouped as one entity. A person confirms the grouping, can mark a pair as not the same, and can undo a merge.

4

Compare accounts

Select two to four accounts to see combined totals with each account side by side. Money moving between the chosen accounts is listed on its own, so transfers within the group are not mistaken for outside flow.

5

Laundering flags

Patterns worth a closer look, by pattern or by party, with amount bands and the transactions behind each flag. Open a flag, add it to a case, or dismiss it. A flag is a prompt for review, not proof. Thresholds in the sample are demo settings.
