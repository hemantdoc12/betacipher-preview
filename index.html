Questions

What is BetaCipher?
A bank statement review tool for investigation teams. It groups statements by account holder and bank, gives single-account views, compares two to four accounts, keeps cases with an activity log, and exports to Excel.
Who is it for?
Investigators and teams who handle bank statements after a search or seizure.
Can I see it working?
Yes. Request a guided walkthrough by email. It is shown on sample data, and we do not need any case files.
Does BetaCipher decide what is suspicious?
No. Flags are prompts for review, not proof. A person reviews each one and decides.
