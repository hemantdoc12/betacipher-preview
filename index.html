BetaCipher

Bank statements, organised for your investigation.

A search brings in a pile of bank statements. BetaCipher helps teams turn them into a view they can review and question.

Designed to help with

  • Reviewing one account: periods, parties and the direction of money.
  • Seeing how funds move across several accounts, with entities grouped.
  • Pointing a reviewer to entries worth a closer look.
  • Exporting results to Excel.

A flag is a prompt for review, not proof.
